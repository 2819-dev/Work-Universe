About QuickSnip

QuickSnip started with a simple problem: community moderators write the same welcomes, reminders and warnings dozens of times a day.

Retyping them is slow, and copying from old messages is error-prone. QuickSnip keeps those replies organized in folders in your Mac's menu bar, ready to copy with a click or a keyboard shortcut, so you can respond quickly and consistently.

It's built to be simple: no account, no subscription and nothing to configure before you can start.

Privacy

Your snippets and Quick Snippets are stored only on your Mac. QuickSnip doesn't have accounts, doesn't collect analytics and never reads, records or uploads anything you copy. The only time it connects to the internet is to check whether a new version is available.
