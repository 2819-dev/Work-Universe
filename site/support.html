Getting started

Install QuickSnip

  1. Download QuickSnip and double-click the file in your Downloads folder.
  2. Drag QuickSnip into your Applications folder and double-click it.
  3. When macOS says it can't verify QuickSnip, click Done. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Copy a snippet

Click the scissors in your menu bar, open a folder and click a snippet. It's copied, ready to paste with ⌘ Command-V. You can also press Control-Option-S in any app to choose a snippet from a menu at your pointer.

Add folders, snippets and Quick Snippets

On the main page, the blue + button adds a folder or a Quick Snippet. Inside a folder, hover over the + button to add a snippet or a subfolder. Right-click anything to rename or delete it.

Give something its own keyboard shortcut

Right-click a folder, subfolder, snippet or Quick Snippet and choose Keyboard Shortcut…, then press the keys you want. Folder shortcuts open the folder, snippet shortcuts copy the snippet instantly, and Quick Snippet shortcuts open the notepad. See them all under ••• → Keyboard Shortcuts.

Common questions

I don't see the scissors in my menu bar.

Your menu bar may be full. On MacBooks with a camera notch, macOS hides icons that don't fit. Quit another menu bar app to make room. The keyboard shortcut always works.

"Open Anyway" isn't showing in Privacy & Security.

It only appears for a short while after you try to open QuickSnip. Double-click QuickSnip in Applications again, then return to Privacy & Security.

A keyboard shortcut doesn't do anything.

Another app may be using the same combination. Open ••• → Keyboard Shortcuts in QuickSnip and choose a different one.

How do I update QuickSnip?

QuickSnip checks for updates automatically. When one is ready, click Update at the bottom of the QuickSnip panel and it installs and reopens by itself. You can also choose ••• → Check for Updates.

The update couldn't be installed.

QuickSnip can only update itself when it's in your Applications folder. Move it there and try again, or download the latest version from this site.

Where are my snippets saved? How do I back them up?

Your snippets are saved only on your Mac. In QuickSnip, choose ••• → Show Library in Finder to see the folder, and copy it somewhere safe to back it up.

I used Snippet Menu before. Where did it go?

Snippet Menu is now QuickSnip. When you update, the app is renamed automatically, and your snippets, settings and keyboard shortcut come with it.

How do I start QuickSnip automatically?

In QuickSnip, choose ••• → Open at Login. If it doesn't stick, open System Settings → General → Login Items, click + under "Open at Login" and choose QuickSnip.

How do I uninstall QuickSnip?

Choose ••• → Quit QuickSnip, then drag QuickSnip from Applications to the Trash. To also delete your snippets, in Finder choose Go → Go to Folder, enter ~/Library/Application Support/QuickSnip and move that folder to the Trash.
