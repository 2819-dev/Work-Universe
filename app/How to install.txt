QUICKSNIP
Your saved replies, one click away.


INSTALL

1. Drag "QuickSnip" into your Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click "QuickSnip".


THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

QuickSnip is distributed independently rather than through the App Store,
so macOS asks you to confirm once:

1. When macOS says it can't verify QuickSnip, click "Done".
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to "Security" and click "Open Anyway".
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway".

A scissors icon appears in your menu bar and the QuickSnip panel opens.


USING IT

- Click the scissors in the menu bar to open the QuickSnip panel.
- Click a folder, then click a snippet to copy it.
- Press Command + V to paste it anywhere.
- Press Control + Option + S in any app for a quick menu at your pointer.
- Use the blue + button to add folders, subfolders, snippets and Quick Snippets.
- Right-click anything to rename, delete or give it its own keyboard shortcut.
- Click the ••• button for settings, About and Support.
- When an update is available, click "Update" at the bottom of the panel.
