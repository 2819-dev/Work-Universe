SNIPPET MENU: HOW TO INSTALL
============================

1. Drag "Snippet Menu" (the app with the scissors icon) into your
   Applications folder.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "Snippet Menu".

   macOS will say it "cannot verify" the app or that it "can't be opened".
   That's expected: the app is free and isn't registered with Apple
   ($99/year). To allow it, just once:

     a. Click "Done" (or "OK" / "Cancel") on that message.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down to the "Security" section. You'll see
        "Snippet Menu was blocked...". Click "Open Anyway".
     d. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.

3. A scissors icon appears in your menu bar (top right of the screen).
   You're done!

EVERYDAY USE
- Press Control + Option + S in any app (or click the scissors icon).
- Pick a category, then a snippet. Its text is copied.
- Click where you want it and press Command + V to paste.

EDITING YOUR SNIPPETS
- Scissors menu > "Edit snippets..." opens your snippets in TextEdit.
- Save, and the menu updates the next time you open it.

START AUTOMATICALLY
- Scissors menu > "Open at Login".
