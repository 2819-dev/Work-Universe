SNIPPET MENU
Your saved replies, one click away.


INSTALL

1. Drag "Snippet Menu" into your Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click "Snippet Menu".


THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

Snippet Menu is distributed independently rather than through the App Store,
so macOS asks you to confirm once:

1. When macOS says it can't verify Snippet Menu, click "Done".
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to "Security" and click "Open Anyway".
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway".

A scissors icon appears in your menu bar and your snippets panel opens.


USING IT

- Click the scissors in the menu bar to open your snippets panel.
- Click a category, then click a snippet to copy it.
- Press Command + V to paste it anywhere.
- Press Control + Option + S in any app for a quick menu at your pointer.
- Inside a category, hover over the blue + button to add a snippet or subcategory.
- Right-click anything to rename, edit or delete it.
- Click the ••• button for settings, including Open at Login.
- When an update is available, click "Update" at the bottom of the panel.
